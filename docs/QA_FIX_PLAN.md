# QA fix plan

Source: QA of the Docker stack on 31 October 2026 — the mobile web build and admin web app in Chrome, and the mobile app on an Android 17 emulator (Expo Go).
Branch: `feature/qa-fixes`. Related changes land together; unrelated ones get separate commits. A step is committed only after:

1. Automated tests for the changed logic pass (`npm test`, plus API e2e where the database is involved).
2. Type check and lint pass.
3. The change is checked manually in Chrome and, for mobile behaviour, on the Android emulator against the running Docker stack.

Logic that lives in screens is moved into `services/` or `packages/shared` where it can be unit tested. The mobile app has no component-rendering test setup, so screen behaviour is verified in the browser and emulator.

### Phase 2 design

Rules followed: CGST Rule 46 — a serial number of at most 16 characters (letters, digits, `-`, `/`), unique for a financial year; supplier name, address and GSTIN; recipient GSTIN if registered; HSN code; rate and amount of each tax head; place of supply for inter-state supplies.

Decisions agreed on 31 October 2026:

- **Numbers** live in new columns `documentNumber`, `financialYear`, `documentSeq` on sales and purchases. The existing `invoiceNumber` stays a free-text reference: for sales a manual reference, for purchases the supplier's invoice number. Numbers cannot be edited.
- **Allocation** happens inside the existing create transaction, after stock movements, by incrementing a `document_counters` row (business, series, financial year) with `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE … RETURNING`. A rolled-back or duplicate request uses no number, so numbering has no gaps.
- **Financial year** comes from the bill date in Asia/Kolkata (1 April–31 March). Bill dates more than a day in the future are rejected, and a numbered bill's date cannot move to another financial year.
- **Prefix**: 1–3 letters or digits, separate for sales (default `INV`) and purchases (default `PUR`). Changing it continues the same sequence. From 10,000 bills in a year the year is shortened (`INV/26-27/10000`) to stay within 16 characters.
- **Existing bills** stay unnumbered and keep their current labels.
- **On phones** a bill gets its number when it syncs. Until then the receipt says so, and print, PDF and share stay disabled. Local-only mode numbers bills on the device with its own per-year counter.
- **GST profile**: shop GSTIN (validated, including the check digit) and state; GSTIN for customers and suppliers. Clearing a field stores no value.
- **Invoice details are copied onto each sale** when it is created: seller GSTIN and state, buyer GSTIN, place of supply and tax type, and each line's HSN code, so issued invoices never change.
- **Tax split**: CGST = half of each line's stored tax (half-up to the paisa), SGST = the rest; IGST when the place of supply differs from the shop's state. Totals are unchanged, so queued offline bills still match.
- **Title**: "Tax invoice" when the shop has a GSTIN, otherwise "Bill of supply".
- **HSN**: an optional code on products (4–8 digits), copied to sale lines.
- **Print**: an A4 HTML invoice with all user text escaped; `expo-print` makes a PDF on phones and the system share sheet sends it; web prints from a sandboxed frame (Save as PDF).

### Phase 3 design

Decisions agreed on 31 October 2026:

- **Discount (16)**: a sale can take one bill-level discount, entered as an amount or as a percentage of the subtotal. A discount given at the time of sale lowers the taxable value, so it is split across the lines in proportion to their amounts (largest remainder, to the paisa) and each line's GST is charged on its discounted amount. Sales store the discount total and the percentage when one was entered; lines store their share. One shared function does the split for the API, phones (offline bills) and admin, so totals always match. Receipts and invoices show the discount; product revenue on the dashboard is net of it. Purchases are unchanged.
- **Walk-in customer (17)**: every shop has one built-in "Walk-in customer", created for existing shops by the migration and for new shops on creation. Quick bills use it when no customer is chosen. It cannot be edited, deactivated or given a GSTIN, and a sale to it must be paid in full (credit needs a named customer). Its sales are counter sales, with the shop's state as the place of supply.
- **Cancel (18)**: owners and admins can cancel a completed sale or purchase with a reason (new permissions `sales.cancel` and `purchases.cancel`). The bill keeps its number and stays in history as Cancelled; totals, dashboards and pending payments leave it out. Stock is reversed through the inventory engine: a sale's stock comes back; a purchase's stock goes out, and cancelling is refused when that stock has already been sold. Money already received or paid is refunded in the same step: a refund entry in the chosen method (default: how it was paid) brings the amount paid back to zero. Cancelling needs a connection. Credit notes for invoices already reported in a GST return are out of scope.
- **Dates (19)**: Record payment takes the payment date (default today; not before the bill date and not in the future). The purchase review step shows and edits the purchase date, the supplier's bill date, which also sets the purchase number's financial year.

### Phase 3b design

Decisions agreed on 31 October 2026:

- **Discard (F1)**: the header button that looked like a menu only opened the discard dialog. It becomes a labelled "Discard" action, and the dialog says what is lost. Leaving with unsaved items still asks first. The same applies to every screen with this pattern.
- **Create product (F2)**: after saving a new product the app returns to the catalog list with "Product added", and the new product is easy to find. The create form gets an optional opening stock field, so stock is not forgotten; editing a product still stays on its page.
- **Tiles (F3)**: the grid becomes two-per-row tiles with a large photo (or the product's initials), the name, the price per unit and a stock badge (available, low, out of stock). The list view and the remembered view choice stay.
- **Stock adjustment (F4)**: from Inventory (phone) and the admin Inventory section, for users with `inventory.adjust`. Modes: set the counted quantity (the app records the difference), add, or remove, with a reason (count correction, damage, expired, other) and a note. It uses the existing `POST /inventory/adjustments` movement types and shows in stock history. Stock cannot go below zero. Phones need a connection. A count sends the stock it was worked out from (`expectedQuantity`); the API refuses it (409) if stock moved in between, and phones sync pending changes first. Damage and expiry only remove stock; "Other" needs a note. For a product kept in groups of pieces (dozen, or a box or pack with a set size), the adjustment is worked out and sent in whole pieces (`unit: "PIECE"`, with `expectedQuantity` in pieces), because after a single piece is sold the stock is no longer a whole thousandth of the unit (23 pieces = 1.91666… dozen).
- **GST optional (F5)**: each new sale and purchase has a "Charge GST" switch (for purchases "Supplier charged GST"), off by default on every screen. Off: no GST on any line (tax 0), and a sale prints as a Bill of supply with the same invoice numbers. On: GST at each product's rate as before; a sale from a shop with a GSTIN is a Tax invoice. A shop without a GSTIN cannot turn GST on for sales (the switch explains why). The API takes `chargeGst`; when it is missing, GST is charged as before, so bills queued by older app versions keep their totals. The choice is stored on the bill, and receipts, invoices, totals, offline bills and admin all follow it. Stored as `chargeGst` (existing bills: true); only an explicit `chargeGst: true` from a shop without a GSTIN is refused, so older queued bills still sync. A no-GST sale keeps its place of supply and tax type; clients title it with the shared `saleDocumentTitle` (Bill of supply when GST is off or the shop has no GSTIN). Phone: drafts keep the switch, "Repeat sale" keeps the repeated bill's choice, and a pending bill without GST is not held back when a product's GST rate changes. Admin: the sale creator's review step and the new-purchase form have the same checkbox and totals preview; the Sales table shows a "Bill type" column (Tax invoice / Bill of supply) and the Purchases table a "GST" column.
- **Returns (F6)**: from a completed sale, return some or all of the quantity on chosen lines (never more than sold minus already returned). Stock comes back (RETURN_IN), and each line's discount share and GST are reversed in proportion. The money goes back as a refund entry in the chosen method, or reduces what the customer still owes. Each sales return is a credit note numbered `CN/2026-27/0001`; a purchase return (RETURN_OUT, refused if the stock is no longer there) is a debit note `DN/2026-27/0001`. Both series restart each financial year like invoices. The original bill never changes; it shows returned quantities and its net amount. Dashboards and reports are net of returns. Returns can be printed and shared like invoices. Owners and admins only (`sales.return`, `purchases.return`), online only. API: `POST /sales/:id/returns` and `POST /purchases/:id/returns` with `requestId`, `items` (`itemId`, `quantity` in the line's unit), optional `reason` and `refundMethod`; they return the bill with its `returns`. A return is dated when it is recorded and numbered in that financial year (CN and DN prefixes are fixed). Its value first reduces what is still owed on the bill and the rest is refunded (default: how the bill was paid). Bills carry `returnedTotal`, `netTotal` and each line's `returnedQuantity`; payments are limited to the net amount. Dashboards count each bill at its net amount on its own date. A bill with returns cannot be cancelled. Sale lines store the stock they took (`stockQuantity`, filled for older lines from the ledger) so a return puts back exactly its share; a part that is not whole pieces is refused. Phone: "Return items" on a saved receipt previews the note's value and how it is settled before it is issued, then opens the new note; lists, Activity and payment balances use the net amount, and a bill's credit or debit note numbers find it in search. Admin: "Return items…" in a completed bill's edit panel (same preview and settlement, refund method when money goes back), the panel lists the bill's notes, and Cancel is not offered once a bill has returns; the Sales and Purchases tables get a Returns column (value and note numbers) and a Credit notes / Debit notes table below them. Sale and purchase list responses include each bill's `returns` for this.
- **Edit items (F7)**: owners and admins can change the items, quantities, prices, discount and GST choice of a completed sale or purchase, giving a reason. This replaces the earlier rule that saved items never change (ADR-011). In one transaction the old stock movements are reversed and the new ones applied (refused if stock is short); totals are recomputed exactly as for a new bill, and the number and date stay. If the new total is below the amount already paid, the edit form records a refund for the difference, in the method chosen. Bills that are cancelled or have returns cannot be edited. Each edit raises the bill's revision and stores who, when and why, shown on the receipt as "Edited". Online only. API: `PUT /sales/:id/items` and `PUT /purchases/:id/items` (permissions `sales.edit` and `purchases.edit`, owners and admins; purchases also need `pricing.view`) take `requestId`, `reason`, `chargeGst` (required), `items` as for a new bill, a sale's `discountAmount` or `discountPercent`, and optional `revision` (the bill revision the form was opened at; 409 if it changed), `expectedTotal` (409 if prices changed it), `expectedRefund` (the refund the form showed, `0.00` when none; 409 if a payment recorded since changes it, since recording a payment does not raise the revision; both clients always send it) and `refundMethod` (default: how it was paid); they return the bill with `revision`, `editedAt`, `editedByUserId` and `editReason`. Customer, supplier and payment method stay; a sale's GST details are copied again from the shop and customer, as for a new bill. Stock moves by the difference per product (ledger notes `Edited sale <id>` / `Edited purchase <id>` for stock that comes back or goes out), so a purchase whose stock was partly sold can still be lowered while that much is in stock. Products already on the bill may stay even if they were deactivated or are not on the supplier's list since. A walk-in sale must stay paid in full: an edit cannot raise its total above what was paid (shared `WALK_IN_EDIT_MESSAGE`). Clients use the shared `billEditBlockReason` and `billEditSettlement` to decide whether Edit is offered and to preview the refund. Phone: "Edit items" on a saved receipt opens a full-screen form started from the server's copy (lines, payments, revision): quantities and prices per line (a selling option keeps its current price), remove lines, add products by search (for purchases only those the supplier offers), the discount and GST switch, then the total before and after, what is refunded (method chosen) or left to collect, and a required reason; the receipt shows "Edited" with the date, time and reason. Admin: "Edit items…" in a sale's or purchase's edit panel (same permissions; not offered on cancelled bills or bills with returns) loads the server's copy and offers the same lines, search, discount, GST switch, before/after totals, refund method and required reason; the panel shows "Edited" with the date, time and reason, and the bills table has an Edited column.
- **Compact headers (F8)** (reported 1 November 2026: on phones the header area took almost half the screen): on every phone list screen (Overview, Catalog, Sales, Purchases, People, Inventory, Activity, a customer's or supplier's history) the title and the main actions share one row: the back button only on screens outside the tab bar (Inventory, Activity, contact history), the title, then labelled icon buttons with 44 dp touch targets and screen-reader names (add, view toggle, adjust stock, all activity). Subtitles that repeat the title or a count shown elsewhere are dropped. Summary cards become one slim strip of figures that scrolls away with the list (four or more figures go two to a row, a single row shares its width by the size of each figure, and the figures' font size is worked out from the strip's width so amounts in lakhs and crores show in full, also on web); search, the Filters button and the quick-filter chips stay pinned at the top while the list scrolls. Quick filters are one horizontal chip row (Sales: All / Unpaid with the amount to collect; Activity: All / Sales / Purchases); the rest go into a Filters bottom sheet whose button shows how many are on (Activity: status and dates; Catalog keeps its sheet), and each active sheet filter also shows as a chip that clears it (Catalog: stock status, unit and inactive products; sort and grouping already show on their own button). Overview shows today's sales and purchases as one two-part strip and puts New sale and All activity in the header row; the low-stock alert card is dropped because the Low stock list right below says the same. Contact history (titled Customer or Supplier) merges the contact card and the balance card into a strip, with Call, WhatsApp and Edit as header icon buttons and the footer actions on one row. The new sale and new purchase forms drop the "Draft · sell/buy prices" subtitle (the review step keeps its date) and use a slimmer step bar. The shared pieces live in `components/screen-heading.tsx` (`ListHeader`, `HeaderIconButton`, `StickySearch`, `ChipRow`, `StatStrip`, `FilterSheet`) and the labels in `services/list-header.ts`. Target, measured on a 1080x2400 phone: the first list row starts within about the top quarter of the screen.

### Phase 6 design

- **Access confirmations and role picker (29)**: on web and phone, making a member an owner ("Make Ravi an owner?"), adding an account as an owner, and taking owner away from someone ("Remove owner access from Sita?", a decision added here because it is as hard to undo) ask first; other role changes apply straight away. Deactivating a membership always asks ("Deactivate Ravi? … Their bills and history stay"); reactivating does not. Deleting a role asks ("Delete the Cashier role? … cannot be undone"), and while any membership, active or not, still has the role it explains who has it instead of offering Delete, since the server refuses that too. Role pickers show role names, never keys, with one line on what each role can do (built-in roles have a fixed description; custom roles show "Custom role · 3 of 42 permissions"), and only offer roles the member may give (Owner only for owners, never more than their own permissions). Web: each member's role select marks the current role, describes the chosen one, and saves with "Change role" (or "Keep Salesperson"), instead of saving on every change. Phone: "Change role" opens a list of radio buttons with the descriptions and "Save role"; the add-member role list and the role editor's permissions are radio buttons and checkboxes for screen readers. Both say what changed ("QA Staff is now Manager."). The wording and rules are in `@invento/shared` (`access-changes.ts`).
- **Accessible names and page titles (30)**: icons are drawn with an icon font, so each one is a private-use character; every phone icon now goes through `components/ui/icon.tsx` (and `IconSymbol`), which hides it from screen readers on phones and web, so buttons and tabs are named by their words ("Overview", "Patil Constructions, Sale · INV/…"), and a lint rule stops direct `@expo/vector-icons` imports. Decorative text glyphs (‹, ›, ⌕, ✓, ＋, ▾ and similar) are hidden the same way. Buttons that show only an icon or glyph have a name ("Go back", "Remove Clear Float Glass 5mm", "Add supplier"), toggles use checkbox and switch roles with their state, and every phone form field has a label matching the text above it, not only a placeholder. On web, the browser tab names the open page: the phone app "Sales · Invento" (`services/page-title.ts`, set from the root layout) and admin "Sales · QA Glass & Hardware · Invento admin", "Businesses · Invento admin" or "Sign in · Invento admin" (`src/page-title.ts`). Admin also names each row's Edit button after the record and hides the decorative arrows and brand letter from screen readers.
- **Consistency (31)**: every phone screen uses one back button (`components/back-button.tsx`): a bordered square with ‹, named "Go back" (a return note's says "Back to bill", like its footer button); with no screen to go back to, as on a web page opened directly, it opens the screen's list instead. Sync status and Team and roles get the same heading as other screens, and the profile row, heading and browser tab use the same names ("Team and roles", "Sync status", "New product"). Starting a sale is called "New sale" everywhere (Overview, Sales tab, the screen itself, its buttons "Review sale · 2 items" and "Save sale · ₹…", the leave dialog and the Activity draft); the full form opened from a customer or "Repeat sale" keeps its draft named "Sale form". The Sales tab icon is ₹ instead of $. A saved bill's buttons always read "Print", "Share PDF" (phones), and "Send on WhatsApp" for a sale whose customer has a usable phone number, otherwise "Share as text" (the system share sheet, also for purchases, and on web only where the browser can share); screen readers hear the document and the customer ("Send tax invoice to Patil Constructions on WhatsApp") (`services/share-actions.ts`). The Sales tab's payments card says "₹1,480.00 to collect" or "Nothing to collect", "2 unpaid sales · 1 overdue", and how many older sales without a recorded payment are not counted; a separate "Show only unpaid sales" switch filters the list, kept in the address so Overview's "Payments to collect" always opens it switched on (`services/pending-payments.ts`). A purchase's quantity step says buy rates are before GST and whether GST is added, labels the rate and line total "before GST" while "Supplier charged GST" is on, and shows each line's GST ("GST 18% · ₹64.80 · With GST ₹424.80"), worked out as the bill total (`services/gst.ts`).
- **Selling-option form (32)**: each option on the phone's product form is "Sold as" (piece, pack, box or dozen for counted products; the same kind of measure, such as kg or g, otherwise), then a sentence: "1 pack = [10] pieces" for a pack or box, "1 dozen = 12 pieces", or "[6] pieces" / "[5] kg"; then "Price for 1 pack" and a line "1 pack = 10 pieces for ₹1,700.00 · ₹170.00 per piece". The name on bills is optional and defaults to "Pack of 10", "Dozen", "Single piece", "6 pieces" or "5 kg". A new option starts as a pack for products counted in pieces. Options are stored as before (`unit`, `quantity`, `piecesPerUnit`), so sales and the API are unchanged; an older "2 packs of 10" or "2 dozen" is shown, and saved, as one pack of 20 or 24 pieces at the same price, and anything else in an older format keeps its quantity field until a unit is chosen. Each field and the unit radio buttons are named for screen readers ("Option 2: pieces in 1 box"). The logic is in `services/selling-option-form.ts`.
